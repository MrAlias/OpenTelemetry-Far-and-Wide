Customer resolution (October 27, 2026):
The pricing baseline makes a real HTTP connection attempt to a missing legacy
backend with a two-second timeout, then reads the current price from PostgreSQL.
The fix removes the stale attempt and retains the same successful store lookup.
Actual Grafana waterfalls precede the repeated-request summary. See canonical
presentation/data/resolution.json and docs/customer-resolution.md for current
measurements; these are local demo observations, not production benchmarks.

Economic scenario (October 27, 2026):
Distributed instrumentation ownership versus centralized injector/operator.
One namespace setup, twelve monthly agent releases, thirteen update owners versus
one central owner. No per-investigation or telemetry-bill credit. $13,900 of
first-year capacity and $10,800 recurring for the demo under explicit hypothetical
labor inputs. See docs/instrumentation-savings.md and the editable calculator.
