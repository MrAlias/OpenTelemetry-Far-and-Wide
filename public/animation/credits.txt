OpenTelemetry Far and Wide — concept animation

Talk: Tyler Yahn and Nikola Grcevski.
Dialogue: two distinct male synthetic stand-ins perform the Tyler and Nikola
roles. This is an authored draft conversation, not an actual recording or an
imitation of either presenter's voice.
Visuals: original explanatory diagrams derived from the repository storyboard.
Traces, versions, and transitions are schematic. No measured cost results appear.
The shared Operator integration remains proposed; the animation does not certify
that the demo is complete or that an upstream unified policy API exists.
